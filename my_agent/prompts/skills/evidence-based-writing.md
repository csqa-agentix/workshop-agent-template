name: evidence-based-writing
description: How to write a short report in which every statement is backed by a source, and uncertainty is stated openly.
---
## Rules
- One statement = one source in brackets, e.g. "The launch is on 12 March [meeting_notes.md]".
- Separate **Facts** (seen in a file), **Inferences** (your reasoning from facts – say so) and **Open questions** (not answerable from the files).
- Prefer numbers and names over adjectives. Keep it under one page.

## Layout
1. `# Title` and a one-line summary.
2. `## Facts` – bullets with sources.
3. `## Inferences` – bullets, each starting "Likely …".
4. `## Open questions` – what a human must decide or provide.
